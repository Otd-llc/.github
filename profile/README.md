# One Thousand Drones

**One mind, many machines.**

One Thousand Drones builds brain-to-swarm systems: biometrically scaled neuro-robotic shared control that reads an operator's brain and body signals and scales one person's intent across many autonomous machines. Broken Arrow, Oklahoma.

Our open education arm, the Academy, teaches the hardware and brain-computer-interface fundamentals behind that work, one build at a time. It is free and hands-on.

### Aplomado HID

<a href="https://onethousanddrones.com/#aplomado"><img alt="The Aplomado HID section of the company site: three drones over a hex floor, the decoded intent HOLD, and the Aplomado mark beside its description" src="assets/shot-apex-aplomado-2026-10.webp" width="720"></a>

<sub>The Aplomado HID section on the company site, simulated signals. Captured October 2026.</sub>

A non-invasive EEG brain-computer interface that turns trained motor imagery into supervisory command of a swarm, tested in simulation. The operator's intent becomes high-level directional commands; each machine's own autonomy flies the rest. OIDAT recognizes an operator going down and passes command to a designated successor; it is tested on simulated signals, and the headset is in development.

- **Live demo** · [demo.onethousanddrones.com](https://demo.onethousanddrones.com) · a simulated fleet driven by recorded EEG, in the browser
- **The program** · [onethousanddrones.com/#aplomado](https://onethousanddrones.com/#aplomado) · the headset, the decoder and OIDAT

### Hex Cluster

<a href="https://hex.onethousanddrones.com"><img alt="A Hex Cluster GCS mast build assembling part by part in the configurator" src="assets/loop-hex-gcs-mast.webp" width="720"></a>

A bench mounting standard you print yourself. Every base carries a dovetail on all six edges, so a layout of any size locks together and moves as one piece. The geometry is a free download.
